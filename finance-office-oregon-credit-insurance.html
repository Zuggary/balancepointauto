Credit life and credit disability insurance

If this insurance ends early, any refund must be paid or credited promptly, unless it is below a minimum the state sets.

If you pay the loan off early, the refund must be made within 30 days after the loan ends.

Our summary of the law and state rule quoted below. Your contract says what applies to you.

Optional? When you apply, you must be told in writing that this coverage is optional; if a lender does require insurance as extra security, you may use a policy you already have or another authorized insurer.

“The refund shall be made not later than the 30th day after the loan is terminated.”

Oregon Administrative Rules 836-060-0011(10)(c)

More from the law (4)

“the Director of the Department of Consumer and Business Services shall prescribe a minimum refund and no refund which would be less than such minimum need be made.”
