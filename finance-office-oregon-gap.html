GAP waiver

GAP bought with a personal-use car can be cancelled for a full refund for at least 30 days after purchase, if you received no benefit from it.

This does not cover GAP from a bank or credit union, a GAP insurance policy, or GAP on a loan that is sold to a company with an Oregon consumer finance license.

Our summary of the law quoted below. Your contract says what applies to you.

Optional? For GAP that this rule covers, you cannot be required to buy it to get the loan or to buy or lease the car.

“a period of at least 30 days after the date on which a borrower purchases a guaranteed asset protection waiver … the borrower may cancel ... and receive a full refund of the purchase price”

Oregon Revised Statutes 646A.770(3)
