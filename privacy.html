Nothing you type leaves this browser.

That is the privacy promise. The calculator runs on your device and sends your numbers to no server, ours included.

A calculator for learning. Not financial, legal or credit advice. We recommend no lender.

What we collect

Nothing.

We ask for no name, email, phone, address, account or card number. There is no sign-up and no lead form. The site stores nothing unless you tick Remember, and loads nothing from other sites.

What stays on your device

Only what you choose to keep, and only in this browser.

  • Remember my numbers on this device is off unless you tick it. When on, your numbers are saved in this browser’s local storage, only on this device. Untick it to erase them.
  • Copy a link with these numbers puts your numbers in the part of the link after the # sign. Browsers do not send that part to any server. Anyone you give the link to can see the numbers in it.

Our web host

The company that serves these pages may log basic request data, never your numbers.

Like any website, the host may log an IP address and the time of a visit. Those logs never contain the numbers you type, because the numbers never leave your browser.

Ads

No ads today. We plan to show Google ads later; this page will say so before any ad appears. The site’s ads.txt file names the ad account for that.

Who runs this

One person, independently. No lender or dealer pays for it, and none of them sees your numbers.
