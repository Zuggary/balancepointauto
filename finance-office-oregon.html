Cancelling the car purchase

Oregon has no 3-day right to cancel a car purchase.

But if your personal-use deal depends on a lender's approval, and no lender approves the agreed terms within 10 calendar days after you take the car, the dealer must finance it on those terms or cancel the deal.

Oregon DOJ guidance and the statute quoted below. Your contract says what applies to you.

“There is no 3-day right of rescission for a car purchase or lease, even if you do not yet have final approval of financing.”

Oregon Department of Justice, Buying a Vehicle, “Closing the Deal”

Vehicle service contract

We found no Oregon rule that sets a time limit, a refund amount or a fee limit for cancelling a service contract.

Oregon law says the contract itself must state the terms for cancelling it.

Oregon DOJ guidance and the statute quoted below. Your contract says what applies to you.

Optional? The Oregon Department of Justice calls service contracts optional, and a state rule bars anyone from falsely saying you must buy add-ons to get the car or the loan.

“The service contract shall state the terms, restrictions or conditions governing termination of the service contract by the service contract holder.”

Oregon Revised Statutes 646A.156(7)

More from the law (2)

“Service contracts (or extended warranties) are optional programs.”

Oregon Department of Justice, Service Contracts

“A person may not falsely represent to a consumer that, unless the consumer purchases additional goods, accessories, services, products or insurance, the person will not sell or lease a motor vehicle to the consumer or cannot provide credit or financing for the consumer.”

Oregon Administrative Rules 137-020-0020(3)(L)

  • Who to ask: DCBS Division of Financial Regulation 888-877-4894(for problems with finance and insurance industries)
  • Oregon DOJ Consumer Protection 1-877-877-9392

GAP waiver

GAP bought with a personal-use car can be cancelled for a full refund for at least 30 days after purchase, if you received no benefit from it.

This does not cover GAP from a bank or credit union, a GAP insurance policy, or GAP on a loan that is sold to a company with an Oregon consumer finance license.

Our summary of the law quoted below. Your contract says what applies to you.

Optional? For GAP that this rule covers, you cannot be required to buy it to get the loan or to buy or lease the car.

“a period of at least 30 days after the date on which a borrower purchases a guaranteed asset protection waiver … the borrower may cancel ... and receive a full refund of the purchase price”

Oregon Revised Statutes 646A.770(3)

More from the law (6)

“in connection with a sale of a motor vehicle that is used for personal, family or household purposes”

Oregon Revised Statutes 646A.776 and 646A.773(2)(c)

“ORS 646A.770 to 646A.787 do not apply to: … A state bank, as defined in ORS 706.008, or a credit union, as defined in ORS 723.008”

Oregon Revised Statutes 646A.773(2)(a)-(d)

“An addendum to a finance agreement that is sold or assigned to a licensee, as defined in ORS 725.010, and that is secured by a motor vehicle”

Oregon Revised Statutes 646A.773(2)(a)-(d)

“An insurance policy that an insurer offers under the provisions of the Insurance Code;”

Oregon Revised Statutes 646A.773(2)(a)-(d)

“A debt cancellation contract or debt suspension agreement offered in compliance with 12 C.F.R. 37.2 or 12 C.F.R. part 721, both as in effect on January 1, 2016;”

Oregon Revised Statutes 646A.773(2)(a)-(d)

“purchasing a guaranteed asset protection waiver is not required in order to obtain credit or complete a purchase or lease of a motor vehicle”

Oregon Revised Statutes 646A.776(3)

  • Who to ask: Oregon DOJ Consumer Protection 1-877-877-9392

Credit life and credit disability insurance

If this insurance ends early, any refund must be paid or credited promptly, unless it is below a minimum the state sets.

If you pay the loan off early, the refund must be made within 30 days after the loan ends.

Our summary of the law and state rule quoted below. Your contract says what applies to you.

Optional? When you apply, you must be told in writing that this coverage is optional; if a lender does require insurance as extra security, you may use a policy you already have or another authorized insurer.

“The refund shall be made not later than the 30th day after the loan is terminated.”

Oregon Administrative Rules 836-060-0011(10)(c)

More from the law (4)

“the Director of the Department of Consumer and Business Services shall prescribe a minimum refund and no refund which would be less than such minimum need be made.”

Oregon Revised Statutes 743.378(1)

“of the optional nature of the coverage, the eligibility requirements, and the policy limitations and exclusions.”

Oregon Administrative Rules 836-060-0060(1) and Exhibit 1

“You ARE NOT required to buy credit life insurance or credit health insurance from any particular insurance company or agent.”

Oregon Administrative Rules 836-060-0060(1) and Exhibit 1

“the creditor shall give the debtor the option of furnishing the required amount of insurance through existing policies”

Oregon Administrative Rules 836-060-0011(2)

  • Who to ask: Division of Financial Regulation (part of DCBS) 888-877-4894(for problems with finance and insurance industries)
