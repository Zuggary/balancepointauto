Questions to ask

Questions you can ask. These are not legal facts.

  1. “Is this product optional? Where does the paperwork say so?”
  2. “What is the price of each add-on, and what is my monthly payment without them?”
  3. “Where does this contract say how to cancel, and who pays the refund?”
  4. “If I pay off or refinance the loan early, how is the unused part refunded, and does it go to me or the lender?”
  5. “Is this deal final, or does it depend on a lender approving it? Can I have your return policy in writing?”
  6. “Which cancellation rule applies to this product, and where is it written?”
